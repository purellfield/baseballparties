FAQ

What ages is this for?

Ages 6 and up. Our operator sets the pitching machine speed for each hitter, so first-time swingers and travel-ball players both get good swings.

How much space do we need?

A flat stretch of grass, turf, or driveway about 55 feet long and 15 feet wide. No room at home? We can set up at a nearby park. Not sure your yard fits? Text us a photo and we will let you know.

Do you need power?

Yes, one standard outdoor outlet within reach of the setup. We bring the extension cords.

What do I need to provide?

The space, the guests, and the cake. We bring the cage, machine, radar gun, bats, and balls, and our operator runs everything.
